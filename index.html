Chase Value: xChase+ and the decision cards

Chase rate counts how often a hitter swings at pitches outside the zone and treats every one as the same mistake. This project prices every chase in runs with Statcast data, ranks every qualified hitter, and turns the number into a one-pitch plan a coach can hand to a player.
