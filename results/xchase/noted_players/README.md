# Noted hitters, 2026

Decision cards for the 17 hitters named in the two coaching reports:
the nine whose chase losses look easiest to fix
([actionable_hitters_2026.md](../coaching/actionable_hitters_2026.md)) and the
bottom 10 by xChase+
([bottom10_2026_coaching_notes.md](../coaching/bottom10_2026_coaching_notes.md)).
Every other qualified hitter has the same card in the interactive page,
`results/xchase/player_cards/index.html` (download it and open it in a browser).

Each card is drawn from the catcher's view, to scale, around the hitter's own
strike zone; nobody is mirrored, so a left-handed batter's inside is on the right.
It compares the hitter with the average hitter on pitches at the same spot
relative to their zone, in the same count and the same batter-side and
pitcher-hand matchup.
Red is where they swing more, blue where they take more, and faded color
means too few pitches to say. The plan is written to the player; the coach's
note underneath is for the staff. For what a normal card looks like, compare
with the average hitter:

**Does taking more pay?** Across 941 hitters' back-to-back seasons, a 10-point cut in far-off chase rate came with +3.1 pts of walk rate, -1.7 pts of strikeout rate and +22 points of xwOBA. Those hitters also swung at 2.5 pts fewer strikes, so keep swinging at strikes while you take the far-off pitch.

## Easiest to improve

Their chases turn out about as well as an average hitter's or better, and the pattern repeats across seasons, so a take rule is the whole adjustment.

### Salvador Pérez

xChase+ 73 (255 of 261). Runs at stake on far-off pitches: 16.9 per 600 PA. Choices −23.3, contact +7.8.

**What to work on**

- With two strikes, let breaking balls below the zone go. You swung at 78% of them; hitters on average swing at 49%.
- With two strikes against right-handers, let breaking balls off the outer edge go. You swung at 74% of them; hitters on average swing at 46%.

**Coach's note.** Volume, not contact: these chases turn out better than the league's, which softens every leak but erases none. Start with breaking balls before two strikes, where the take costs nothing in strikeouts.
