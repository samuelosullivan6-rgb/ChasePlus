Chase decisions by team, 2025

Every qualified hitter, sorted by the runs his swing choices on far-off pitches cost per 600 plate appearances. Click a name for his decision card. Beat is the number to get under in 2026: what the frozen line expects with no change at all, with this season's number beside it. On a phone, tap a hitter for the rest of his row.

Runs are per 600 plate appearances. Choices is what the swing decisions cost against an average hitter at the same spot, count and matchup; contact is what the chases produced compared with the average hitter's chases. Problem: the share of the loss that is choices (step 13). Top leak: the costliest count, pitcher hand, pitch group and side of the zone, with the hitter's rate against the league's and how far off the zone the chased pitches were (median inches). Count check: how he hits once the count reaches the leak's counts, shrunk for noise, against the league in the same counts. Beat: leak chasing in points above the league that the frozen line expects next season with no change, with this season's number beside it (step 19); a fix is anything under it. Start with: the plan's first pitch line, with the two rates and the inches off; the rest of the plan is on the card.
