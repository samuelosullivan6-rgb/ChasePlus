Every qualified hitter, sorted by the runs his swing choices on far-off pitches cost per 600 plate appearances. Click a name for his decision card. Choices is what the swing decisions cost against an average hitter at the same spot, count and matchup; contact is what the chases produced compared with the average hitter's chases. Problem: the share of the loss that is choices (step 13). Top leak: the costliest count, pitch group and side of the zone, with the hitter's rate against the league's and how far off the zone the chased pitches were (median inches). Count check: how he hits once the count reaches the leak's counts, shrunk for noise, against the league in the same counts. Target: leak chasing in points above the league, this season → next season with no change (step 19). Start with: the first line of the plan; the rest is on the card.
